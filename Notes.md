# General Gameplay Ideas
- Progressive unlock of combat e.g. melee magnetism, damage, animation, combo/chain attacks
- Zombies have body part scripts - progressively disable zombies - need animations e.g. arm crawl, limp
- Ragdoll physics for damaged limbs
- Progessively disable character body parts - imagine dragging yourself while zombies start eating you

# Story Ideas
- Secure Pueblo campus by progressively unlocking sections
- Game begins with player being chased by a horde.
